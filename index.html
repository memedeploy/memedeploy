Features

Everything your launch needs

No wallet connection

You never expose a private key. Just send SOL to the displayed address from any compatible Solana wallet.

We create the pool for you. Your chosen SOL is paired with 20% of the total supply to seed trading instantly.

Market maker bot

Generate early buy orders from rotating wallets to build volume and attract organic investors on launch day.

On-chain metadata

Token name, description, and all social links are embedded directly into on-chain metadata from day one.

Fast deployment

Typical on-chain confirmation takes under 30 seconds. Your token appears in wallets and explorers immediately.

Transparent fees

A flat 0.15 SOL listing fee plus your chosen liquidity. No hidden charges, no subscription, no surprises.
